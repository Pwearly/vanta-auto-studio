06 — A few good questionsClarity is part of the service
Before we begin

GOOD TO
KNOW.

No exaggerated promises. Here's what to expect before a vehicle comes into the studio.

Ask something else ↗
Can every scratch be removed?+

No. Some defects are too deep to safely polish out. A proper inspection determines what can be improved without compromising the clear coat.

How long will the work take?+

It depends on the vehicle's size, condition and selected process. We confirm a realistic time window after understanding the work required.

Is ceramic protection permanent?+

No coating is permanent or maintenance-free. Durability depends on the product, preparation, usage and care routine. The exact product and expected maintenance should be discussed in the quote.

Why does the estimate say “illustrative”?+

This is a website demo. The displayed figures are sample values used to demonstrate the interactive quote builder and must be replaced with the business's real pricing before the site goes live.

What should I send for a more accurate quote?+

Share the make, model, year, vehicle category, the condition of the paint and the result you're hoping for. Clear photos can help with an initial conversation, but a final scope may require an inspection.
